# KYC Lens community conduct

This project welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience, and perspectives. These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

- Treat people with respect. Critique ideas and code with specific, constructive feedback.
- Make room for questions, mistakes, and disagreement. Assume good intent and respond to the substance of a contribution.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or demean others. Sexualized content and unwanted personal attention are not welcome.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information, identity documents, face media, or private communications without their consent.
- Follow the scope and moderation decisions of the project space.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, and limit or end participation when needed. Responses should consider the severity and pattern of the behavior and explain the decision when practical.

Report a visible concern to a maintainer in the relevant project thread when doing so is safe, or use GitHub's reporting tools. If a maintainer has published a private contact method on their profile or in the repository, use it for concerns that should remain private. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public report. This project has no dedicated private conduct contact until the repository owner publishes one.
